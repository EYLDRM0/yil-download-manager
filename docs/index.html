YIL Download Manager

A Chrome extension that finds and downloads the media on the page you are viewing.
Görüntülediğiniz sayfadaki medyayı bulan ve indiren bir Chrome eklentisi.

  • Lists video and audio files and HLS streams as you browse
  • Saves them with one click, from the toolbar or a button over the video
  • “Download with YDM” in the right-click menu
  • English and Turkish interface
Privacy policy / Gizlilik politikası · Terms of use / Kullanım koşulları · Support / Destek

Download only content you have the right to download. DRM-protected content and YouTube are not supported.
